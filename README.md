# Dalitiwan Store – Inventory Management System

A static, frontend-only Inventory Management System for **Dalitiwan Store** located in **Bakia, Majayjay, Laguna, Philippines**.

## Business Details
- **Business Name:** Dalitiwan Store
- **Business Type:** Sari-Sari Store
- **Location:** Bakia, Majayjay, Laguna, Philippines
- **Operating Hours:** 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- **Contact Person:** Precy Carabuena

## Included Pages
- `index.html` — Dashboard (KPI cards, Recent Inventory Activity, Inventory Summary, Quick Actions)
- `products.html` — Product Management (Search, Category Filter, Add/Edit Modal, Delete Confirmation, Mobile Cards)
- `inventory.html` — Inventory Management (Stock In / Stock Out adjustments, Low Stock Alerts, Stock Movement History)
- `reports.html` — Reports (Low Stock Report, Out-of-Stock Report, Quantity & Category Charts, Summary Table)
- `about.html` — Store Information (Store background, business details, location, hours, contact person)

## How to Run
Extract the ZIP folder and open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No server, backend, database, or login is required.
